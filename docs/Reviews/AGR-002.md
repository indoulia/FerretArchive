## Purpose

This is not another architecture document. It determines whether ARCH-028's proposed amendments to ARCH-025 and ARCH-027 should become part of the frozen V2 Foundation, as the resolution of AGR-001 §5 Deferred Question F5. It reviews ARCH-028 only — the frozen foundation itself is not re-reviewed, and no Closed Architectural Decision (AGR-001 §6) is reopened except to confirm none is contradicted.

---

## Executive Summary

**Verdict: Approved.** ARCH-028 resolves AGR-001 F5 within its declared scope, introduces no implementation mechanism, contradicts no Closed Architectural Decision, and improves the conceptual integrity of the foundation by separating request equivalence, artifact validity, and dependency resolution into three distinct, non-overlapping questions. Three Significant findings were raised during review — all three were corrected in ARCH-028 (now v1.1) before this verdict was reached, and all three corrections were verified against the live repository text. No Critical findings. Both proposed amendments are accepted, with corrected citations.

## 2. Review Methodology

ARCH-028 was assessed against the seven criteria specified for this review. Every finding was verified against the live text of the file it concerns — the two "current text" citations in ARCH-028 §10 were checked character-for-character against the actual live content of ARCH-025 §3 and ARCH-027 §4, not accepted on the strength of ARCH-028's own claim. Findings are classified **Critical**, **Significant**, **Minor**, or **Editorial**, with a disposition of **Required correction**, **Deferred**, or **Leave unchanged**. Where a Required correction was identified and ARCH-028 had not yet been frozen, the correction was applied directly to the draft and re-verified, rather than left open — this is ordinary pre-acceptance revision, not an amendment to the frozen foundation, and required no separate review cycle of its own.

## 4. Criteria Assessment

**1. Does ARCH-028 resolve AGR-001 F5?** Yes. F5's exact text (AGR-001 §5) is "what makes two requests 'the same' for the purpose of identifying a resolution candidate." ARCH-028 §1–§4 define a request, its identity, and an exact equivalence relation over that identity — a direct, complete answer.

**2. Does it remain within its declared scope?** Yes. ARCH-028's Scope section excludes exactly what its Non-Goals (§9) then also exclude, and the document does not stray into either at any point (verified in §5 below).

**3. Does it introduce any implementation mechanism?** No. A full-text search of ARCH-028 for "key," "hash," "fingerprint," "cache," "retriev-," "storage," and "API" found no occurrence used as a mechanism ARCH-028 itself defines — every occurrence is either in the prohibited-terms list (§9) or in a sentence explicitly disclaiming that concern (e.g., "would require a rule for comparing and ranking degrees of overlap, which is a retrieval concern").

**4. Does it preserve every Closed Architectural Decision from AGR-001?** Yes, checked against all nine (AGR-001 §6) individually. Of particular note: Decision 9 ("Resolution is a decision procedure, distinct from retrieval") is not merely preserved but actively reinforced — ARCH-028 §4 and §9 both explicitly name partial/fuzzy equivalence as a retrieval concern and exclude it for that reason. Decision 6 (validity is dependency stability, not output reproducibility) is preserved by §6's explicit separation of equivalence (candidacy) from validity (acceptability) — ARCH-028 does not touch ARCH-025's validity definition itself.

**5. Does it introduce any hidden assumptions?** One was found (G3) and corrected. No others were identified: §5 explicitly separates request identity from dependency shapes 1–4 rather than assuming a relationship; §4 explicitly states equivalence recognizes no partial form rather than leaving degree-of-match undefined; §8's guarantees make the "no cross-inference" and "no side effects" carry-forwards explicit rather than implicit.

**6. Does it improve the conceptual integrity of the V2 Foundation?** Yes. Before ARCH-028, ARCH-027 §4 asserted a request "already knows" its candidate without saying what that means — the single largest hidden assumption AGR-001 identified. ARCH-028 replaces that assumption with a defined relation, and in doing so cleanly separates three questions (is this the same request; is a matched artifact still valid; does it satisfy resolution) that were previously conflated into one sentence.

**7. Are the proposed amendments to ARCH-025 and ARCH-027 sufficient and minimal?** After G1/G2 corrections: yes. Each amendment changes exactly one sentence, adds a cross-reference, and changes nothing else in either document — verified by re-reading both target documents in full for any other place a "request" is used without definition (ARCH-025 line 91, 136, 151 and ARCH-027's remaining "request" occurrences were all checked; none besides the two amended sentences make an undefined claim requiring a fix).
