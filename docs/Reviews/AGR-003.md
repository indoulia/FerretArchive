## Purpose

This is not another architecture document. It determines whether ARCH-029's proposed amendments to ARCH-025 and ARCH-027 should become part of the frozen V2 Foundation, as the resolution of AGR-001 §5 Deferred Question F7. It reviews ARCH-029 only. No Closed Architectural Decision (AGR-001 §6) is reopened — this review confirms none is contradicted.

---

## Executive Summary

**Verdict: Approved.** ARCH-029 resolves AGR-001 F7 by correctly identifying that the "eager vs. lazy" framing AGR-001 used was itself the wrong question — the frozen foundation already rules out an active propagation mechanism (ARCH-023 §9, ARCH-027 §1), leaving consultative, check-time propagation as the only architecturally possible model rather than a choice. Two Significant findings were raised during review — both were corrected in ARCH-029 (now v1.1) before this verdict was reached and verified against the live repository. No Critical findings. Both proposed amendments are accepted as originally written — neither amendment's text itself needed correction, only two clarifications elsewhere in the document.

## 2. Review Methodology

ARCH-029 was assessed against the seven criteria specified for this review, each checked against the live text of the documents it touches rather than accepted on the strength of its own claims. Both "current text" citations in ARCH-029 §9 were verified character-for-character against the live content of ARCH-025 §5 and ARCH-027 §3. Findings are classified **Critical**, **Significant**, **Minor**, or **Editorial**, with a stated correction requirement and a disposition of **Fix now**, **Defer**, or **Leave unchanged**. Where a Significant finding was identified and ARCH-029 had not yet been frozen, the correction was applied directly to the draft and re-verified — ordinary pre-acceptance revision, not an amendment to the frozen foundation.

## 4. Criteria Assessment

**1. Does ARCH-029 resolve the ambiguity identified in AGR-001 F7?** Yes. F7's exact text (AGR-001 §5) asks "whether invalidation propagates eagerly... or lazily." ARCH-029 §1–§3 answer this by showing the question presupposes a choice that does not exist: nothing in the frozen foundation authorizes an eager mechanism, so consultative propagation is the only model available — a stronger and more precise resolution than picking one side of the original framing.

**2. Does it preserve every Closed Architectural Decision?** Yes, checked against all nine (AGR-001 §6) individually. Decision 6 (validity is dependency stability, not output reproducibility) is explicitly preserved — ARCH-029 §5 states outright that "this document does not change what makes an artifact valid." Decision 7 (minimum-invalidation and fail-closed) is extended, not altered — §6's outcome-combination rule uses the same Not-satisfied-outranks-Indeterminate-outranks-Satisfied ordering already established, applied across a set instead of a single link. Decision 9 (resolution is not retrieval) is untouched — ARCH-029 makes no retrieval claim anywhere.

**3. Does it remain architecture-only?** Yes. A search for background-process and mechanism vocabulary ("worker," "queue," "poll," "schedule," "database," "API," "cache," "listener," "thread," any event type name) found every occurrence either in an explicit non-goal/disclaimer or explaining why that mechanism is *not* permitted — never a mechanism the document itself defines.

**4. Does it introduce any hidden assumptions?** Two were found (G1, G2) and corrected. No others were identified: §1 explicitly grounds observability in existing ARCH-023/027 text rather than assuming a new capability; §3 explicitly rules out the model it does not permit rather than leaving it ambiguous; §7 explicitly states no in-progress state exists rather than assuming one implicitly.

**5. Are the proposed amendments to ARCH-025 and ARCH-027 minimal and sufficient?** Yes, unchanged by G1/G2 corrections since neither finding touched §9. Amendment 1 appends one clause and a cross-reference to an existing sentence in ARCH-025 §5; Amendment 2 inserts one new sentence after ARCH-027 §3's existing final sentence, altering none of the three outcome definitions. Both "current text" citations were verified byte-exact against the live documents (ARCH-025:121, ARCH-027:89) before this verdict.

**6. Does it introduce any implementation coupling?** No, once G2 was corrected. Before correction, the unqualified "chain"/"link" vocabulary risked coupling the architecture to an implicit linear-traversal assumption; the correction makes explicit that the document describes a set-level property, not a specific traversal structure.

**7. Does it improve conceptual consistency across the frozen foundation?** Yes. It retires a framing (eager vs. lazy) that never fit the rest of the series, replaces it with a consistency property derived from principles ARCH-023 and ARCH-027 already state, and extends the existing fail-closed ordering (Not-satisfied > Indeterminate > Satisfied) to multi-artifact dependency sets without introducing a competing principle.
