## Purpose

This is not an architecture document. It is the governance record for ARCH-023 through ARCH-027: what was reviewed, how, what was found, what must change before the series is treated as settled, what is deliberately left open, and what is now closed. It redesigns nothing. Every correction and deferral recorded here refers back to the architecture documents themselves — this record does not restate or replace their content.

---

## Executive Summary

**Verdict: Accepted.** ARCH-023 through ARCH-027 form a coherent, internally consistent architectural foundation for Ferret V2. No Critical findings were raised. Mandatory corrections F1–F3 (§4) have been applied and the series is confirmed as the approved V2 foundation. Four further architectural questions are knowingly deferred, not overlooked (§5), and nine architectural decisions are formally closed as of this record (§6). The series is now **frozen** per §8.

## 2. Review Methodology

The series was reviewed as a single architectural system, not as five independent documents, across ten dimensions: terminology consistency, ownership consistency, source-of-truth consistency, dependency-direction consistency, layering consistency, circular dependencies, hidden assumptions, architectural gaps, duplicate concepts, and missing conceptual models.

Every finding below was verified against the exact text of the document it concerns (direct read/search of the source file) before being recorded — no finding rests on a recollection or summary of a document's content. Findings are classified by severity (**Critical**, **Significant**, **Minor**, **Editorial**) and given a disposition (**Fix now**, **Defer**, **Leave unchanged**).

The review performed no redesign, proposed no new feature, and specified no implementation mechanism. Where a finding implies a change, that change is a correction to existing wording or an explicit deferral — never a new architectural decision.

## 4. Required Corrections (Mandatory)

These three corrections must be applied before the series is treated as approved (§7). None of them alter scope, ownership boundaries, or any closed decision (§6) — each is a wording correction to align a document with what the series already, correctly, does everywhere else.

| ID | Document(s) | Correction | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | ARCH-025 (§4, Impact section), ARCH-026 (Impact section) | Every reference to "Specification Engine" or "Memory Engine" must either be dropped or explicitly re-caveated as a non-approved, gap-only term, exactly as ARCH-024 already does | Resolved |
| F2 | ARCH-026 §3 | The "Model/provider configuration identity" row must state that ownership is currently unassigned among the eight approved components — not attributed to Workspace Engine — and recorded as a gap | Resolved |
| F3 | ARCH-023 §6 | Reword to state the relationship precisely: V1 does not require V2 to function, and any call an engine makes into V2's boundary is optional and non-blocking — not that no such call exists | Resolved |

## 5. Deferred Architectural Questions

These four items are knowingly left open. They are not oversights — each was surfaced by this review and is recorded here so no future document silently assumes an answer to them.

| Question | Originating finding | Description | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Request Identity & Equivalence | F5 | What makes two requests "the same" for the purpose of identifying a resolution candidate. This is the most consequential open item — ARCH-027's resolution model has nothing to check without it | The next V2 document that addresses how a request comes to reference a specific prior artifact instance |
| Deletion semantics | F6 | Whether and how source/asset deletion is treated as a distinct invalidation source, separate from modification | A future revision of ARCH-025 §4, or the document that next extends invalidation sources |
| Invalidation propagation timing | F7 | Whether invalidation propagates eagerly (the moment an upstream dependency changes) or lazily (only when a downstream artifact is next resolved) | A future revision of ARCH-025 §5, or ARCH-027's own refinement |
| Validity-class / dependency-shape matrix | F9 | An explicit mapping between the four validity classes and the five dependency shapes ARCH-025 defines | A future revision of ARCH-025 §2–3 |

No future V2 document may treat any of these four as already settled. Each must be either explicitly resolved by a document that cites this record, or re-deferred with a stated reason.

## 6. Closed Architectural Decisions

The following are settled as of this record. Reopening any of them requires a new governance review — no future V2 document may silently redefine, contradict, or re-litigate them.

1. **V2's scope and non-goals** are as stated in ARCH-023 §3–4.
2. **The eight approved component names** — Workspace Engine, Connector Platform, Parser Platform, Index Engine, Knowledge Engine, Review Engine, Artifact Engine, Domain Event Bus — are the only names any V2 document may use as an owning component, once F1 is corrected.
3. **The formal definition of "AI-derived artifact"** (ARCH-023, Definitions) stands as written.
4. **The real persisted-state root is `.ferret/`, not `.ai/`** (ARCH-023 v1.2; ARCH-024 Critical Finding 1).
5. **Review Engine, Specification Engine, Artifact Engine, and Memory Engine have no implementation today** (ARCH-024 Critical Finding 3) is closed as a statement of current fact. Their eventual responsibilities, if ever implemented, remain correctly open — this closure applies only to the present-tense claim.
6. **Validity is defined by dependency stability, not output reproducibility** (ARCH-025 §1) — the load-bearing insight of the series. This must not be revisited.
7. **Minimum-invalidation and fail-closed** are the governing principles for both validity (ARCH-025 §5, §8) and persistence failure (ARCH-026 §7).
8. **Persistence is scoped to reuse candidates only, owned by existing components, and never constitutes a new store** (ARCH-026 §1, §4, §6).
9. **Resolution is a decision procedure, distinct from retrieval** (ARCH-027 §1) — foundational to keeping later documents from drifting into cache or retrieval design.

## 7. Approval Decision

The series is **Accepted**. Findings F4, F8 required no action and are acknowledged as informational. Findings F1, F2, F3 were **mandatory** (§4) and have been applied and confirmed. Findings F5, F6, F7, F9 are **deferred** (§5) and did not block approval — they are recorded conditions, not corrections.

With F1, F2, and F3 applied and verified against source text, ARCH-023 through ARCH-027 are the approved conceptual foundation for all subsequent Ferret V2 architecture work.

---

## 8. Conditions for Freezing the Series

The series is considered **frozen** as of this record, now that all of the following hold:

1. All three mandatory corrections (§4) have been applied to their respective documents and verified against the corrected text.
2. All four deferred questions (§5) are recorded as explicit Open Questions in whichever document each is nearest to, so they are discoverable without this record.
3. This document's Status is "Accepted."

Now that it is frozen:

- **No future V2 document may reopen a Closed Architectural Decision (§6)** by redefining, contradicting, or silently reinterpreting it. Reopening any of them requires a new governance review, not an inline edit to ARCH-023 through ARCH-027.
- **Future V2 documents must cite this record (AGR-001)** as evidence that scope, vocabulary, ownership, and the core conceptual model are settled, rather than re-establishing them.
- **The four deferred questions remain legitimately open** and may be addressed by any future document that explicitly takes ownership of one — this is the one category of change the freeze does not restrict.
- Amendments to ARCH-023 through ARCH-027 after freezing (beyond resolving a deferred question) require the same governance process this record establishes: a review, a finding, a disposition, and a record.
