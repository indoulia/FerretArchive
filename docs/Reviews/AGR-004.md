## Purpose

This is not another architecture document. It determines whether ARCH-030's proposed amendments to ARCH-025 should become part of the frozen V2 Foundation, as the resolution of AGR-001 (Architecture Governance Review: V2 Foundation Series) Deferred Question F6 (Deletion Semantics) and Deferred Question F9 (Validity-Class / Dependency-Shape Matrix). It reviews ARCH-030 only, against the five questions specified for this review. No Closed Architectural Decision (AGR-001 §6) is reopened — this review confirms none is contradicted.

---

## Executive Summary

**Verdict: Approved.** ARCH-030 (Dependency Participation Semantics) resolves both Deferred Question F6 (Deletion Semantics) and Deferred Question F9 (Validity-Class / Dependency-Shape Matrix), preserves every Closed Architectural Decision, remains architecture-only, and leaves Shape 3's deletion ambiguity intentionally and demonstrably bounded rather than unspecified. Three Significant-to-Minor findings were raised during review — all three were corrected in ARCH-030 (now version 1.1) before this verdict was reached, and all three corrections were verified against the live repository. No Critical findings. Both proposed amendments are accepted as originally proposed — neither amendment's target text or insertion point needed correction, only the surrounding body text and one amendment's added sentence needed sharpening.

## 2. Review Methodology

ARCH-030 was assessed against the five questions specified for this review. Every finding was verified against the live text of the file it concerns — both "current text" citations in ARCH-030 §9 (Proposed Amendments) were checked character-for-character against the live content of ARCH-025 §3 and §4, and every matrix cell claiming a basis in ARCH-024 (Artifact Inventory) or ARCH-025 was checked against the actual wording of those documents rather than accepted on ARCH-030's own claim. Findings are classified **Critical**, **Significant**, **Minor**, or **Editorial**, each with a stated recommendation of **Fix now**, **Defer**, or **Leave unchanged**. Where a finding was identified and ARCH-030 had not yet been frozen, the correction was applied directly to the draft and re-verified — ordinary pre-acceptance revision, not an amendment to the frozen foundation.

## 3. Findings

| H1 | Question 1 (complete resolution of F6) | Deferred Question F6 asks whether deletion is treated as "a distinct invalidation source" — language that implies classification within ARCH-025 §4's existing "already observable" / "not currently observable" structure. | H2 | Question 4 (Shape 3 ambiguity intentionally bounded) | ARCH-030's original Shape 3 table row stated the deletion/modification distinction is left unresolved, but never stated what happens in the absence of that distinction — leaving open whether the gap is a bounded, safe default or an unspecified behaviour | Significant | **Fix now — applied.** §2 now states explicitly that, absent a future distinction, Shape 3 changes fall through to the existing modification rule (ARCH-025 §4/§5), which still correctly invalidates — the gap is a difference in guarantee strength, never a lapse into unchecked behaviour |
| H3 | Question 2 (matrix internal consistency) | §5's introduction claimed "every cell is grounded in a real artifact... or an existing statement... no cell is asserted without one." The connection is sound reasoning, but the claim that every cell is a direct restatement overstates it for this and the "Indirect only" cells | Minor | **Fix now — applied.** §5's introduction now distinguishes cells that restate an existing statement from cells that are logical extensions this document draws, and names the `WorkspaceStateDto` cell as an example of the latter |

No Critical findings were identified. No findings remain requiring correction after this review.

## 4. Question-by-Question Assessment

**Does ARCH-030 completely resolve F6 and F9?** Yes, after H1's correction. Deferred Question F6 is resolved by §1 (what a dependency's existence means), §2 (deletion's meaning, its unconditional consequence, its non-uniform applicability across the five dependency shapes, and — post-correction — its explicit placement in ARCH-025 §4's existing gap category). Deferred Question F9 is resolved by §5's matrix, covering all twenty class-by-shape combinations with a stated basis for each.

**Is the matrix internally consistent with ARCH-024 (Artifact Inventory) and ARCH-025 (Artifact Validity Model)?** Yes, after H3's correction. Every "Applicable" and "Excluded" cell was checked against the specific artifact or statement it cites and matches. The "Indirect only" and "Source" cells are sound extensions of established facts, now correctly labelled as such rather than implied to be direct citations.

**Does the deletion model contradict any frozen architectural decision?** No contradiction found, checked against all nine Closed Architectural Decisions (AGR-001 §6) individually. Of particular relevance: Closed Decision 7 (minimum-invalidation and fail-closed) is preserved — deletion's "unconditional Not-satisfied" consequence is still scoped to the specific dependency chain affected, per ARCH-029 (Validity Propagation Architecture) §6's outcome-combination rule, which ARCH-030 §6 (Architectural Termination and Exclusion Rules) correctly cites rather than restates or alters.

**Does Shape 3 ambiguity remain intentionally bounded rather than becoming unspecified behaviour?** Yes, after H2's correction. The gap is now explicitly shown to default to an already-established, safe mechanism (ordinary modification-based invalidation) rather than to undefined behaviour — a difference in the strength of the guarantee, not a hole in it.

**Are the two proposed amendments to ARCH-025 minimal and sufficient?** Yes. Both "current text" citations in ARCH-030 §9 were verified byte-exact against the live ARCH-025 §3 and §4 (version 1.3) before this verdict. Amendment 1 adds one paragraph after §4's existing final paragraph; Amendment 2 adds one paragraph after §3's existing final paragraph. Neither amendment alters any existing sentence in ARCH-025. H1's correction changed the wording of Amendment 1's added paragraph but not its insertion point or scope.

## 5. Approval Decision

**Approved.** ARCH-030 version 1.1 (post-correction) is accepted as the resolution of AGR-001 Deferred Question F6 and Deferred Question F9.

### Amendments Accepted

**Amendment 1 (ARCH-025 §4 "Invalidation Sources," resolves F6)** — accepted as corrected in ARCH-030 version 1.1 §9: appends a paragraph after §4's existing final paragraph, defining deletion as distinct from modification and explicitly placing it in the "not currently observable" category alongside the three gaps already listed there.

**Amendment 2 (ARCH-025 §3 "Dependency Types That Determine Validity," resolves F9)** — accepted exactly as originally proposed in ARCH-030 §9: appends a cross-reference sentence after §3's existing final paragraph, pointing to ARCH-030 for the canonical Validity-Class × Dependency-Shape matrix. No change was needed to this amendment during review.
