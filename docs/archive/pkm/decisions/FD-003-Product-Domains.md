## Title

Product Domains

## Definition

A Product Domain is a stable business responsibility that groups related capabilities. Product Domains are independent of technology, implementation, and organizational structure.

## Governance

- Domain IDs use the format DOM-xxx.
- Every Capability belongs to exactly one primary Domain.
- Domains are expected to remain highly stable.
- New Domains require explicit ARB approval.
