# FD-001: Definition of a Product Principle

**Scope:** PKM v0.1
**Status:** Approved

---

## Title

Definition of a Product Principle

## Definition

A Product Principle is a long-lived architectural belief that guides product decisions across all capabilities. It is technology-independent, customer-oriented, and expected to remain valid for many years.

## Inclusion Criteria

- Stable for years
- Applies across multiple capabilities
- Helps resolve architectural decisions
- Independent of implementation technologies
- Expresses product intent

## Exclusion Criteria

A Product Principle is NOT:

- Feature
- Roadmap item
- Coding guideline
- Implementation detail
- Release goal
- Temporary strategy

## Governance

- Principle IDs use PR-xxx
- Principles are expected to be highly stable
- Adding a new principle requires explicit architectural review
