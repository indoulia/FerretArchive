# Long-Term Vision — Ambient Context OS

**Status:** Directional only. Not roadmapped in detail; not approved.

## Theme

Ferret's boundary of who can *extend* it opens fully — this is [FEP-001](../../FEP/FEP-001-Product-Architecture.md) Generation 4 (Ecosystem) at maturity. New source types, new structure types, and new consumer surfaces are added predominantly by third parties, not exclusively by Ferret's own engineering team.

## Directional Themes (Not Committed Capabilities)

- **Full third-party ecosystem.** The Extension Marketplace (started in v4) matures into a genuine open extension surface for both sources and consumers, per [FEP-001 §7 Generation 4](../../FEP/FEP-001-Product-Architecture.md).
- **Ubiquitous workspace surfaces.** Context delivery reaches every surface a consumer already works in (IDEs, chat platforms, CI systems) rather than requiring the consumer to come to Ferret.
- **Subscription-driven delivery as the default interaction shape.** Consumers increasingly receive relevant context proactively (subscribed, per [FEP-001 §6](../../FEP/FEP-001-Product-Architecture.md)) rather than only pulling it on request.
- **Federation without a practical edge.** The distinction between "internal workspace" and "external workspace" continues to erode as cross-organization federation (v4) matures and network effects compound.

## What This Explicitly Does Not Include

Nothing here reopens Ferret's Non-Goals ([FEP-001 §1.3, §5.2](../../FEP/FEP-001-Product-Architecture.md)). Ambient reach and ecosystem openness describe *acquisition, organization, and delivery* becoming ubiquitous — not Ferret beginning to reason over the context it delivers.

## Purpose of This Document

To hold long-horizon directional themes so they aren't lost, without pretending they are scoped, sequenced, or approved. Promotion to a real generation (v5+) requires the same lifecycle as any other roadmap item — see [README.md](../README.md#promotion-lifecycle).
