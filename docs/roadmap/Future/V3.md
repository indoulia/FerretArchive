# Ferret v3 — Trust & Enterprise Governance

**Status:** Roadmapped, not approved. No FEP prompt has been issued for this generation.

## Theme

Context stops being merely *available* and becomes *defensible*. This generation matures [FEP-001](../../FEP/FEP-001-Product-Architecture.md)'s Generation 2 (Trust) — Provenance & Attribution and Access Control & Policy — from "present" to "complete."

## Candidate Capabilities

- **Context Health & Trust Surfacing.** A delivery surface (dashboard) showing freshness, provenance completeness, and coverage gaps directly — not something a consumer has to infer from raw provenance records. Elevates [CAP-07](../../FEP/capabilities/FEP-002-CAP-07-Provenance-Attribution.md) and [CAP-10](../../FEP/capabilities/FEP-002-CAP-10-Observability-Health.md) to a first-class product surface.
- **Enterprise Access Governance.** Full RBAC including an AI-agent principal type, SSO/identity-provider integration, and audit logging of access decisions — the compliance gate most enterprise procurement requires. Matures [CAP-08](../../FEP/capabilities/FEP-002-CAP-08-Access-Control-Policy.md). Directly picks up the RBAC deferral in [Deferred-Scope.md](Deferred-Scope.md).
- **Usage & Cost Analytics.** Dashboards over the Usage Ledger (already designed for v2.0): what's queried, by whom, at what token cost. Extends [CAP-10](../../FEP/capabilities/FEP-002-CAP-10-Observability-Health.md).

## Why Now (Directionally)

Sequenced after v2.0 because trust and governance maturity is most valuable once there's federated, multi-workspace content worth governing — governing a single repo's access is a materially smaller problem than governing a graph of referenced workspaces.

## Explicitly Not This Generation

Cross-organization federation ("Ferret Hub") and a public extension marketplace — see [V4.md](V4.md) and [LONG-TERM.md](LONG-TERM.md).
