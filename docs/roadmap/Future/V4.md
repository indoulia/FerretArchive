# Ferret v4 — Federation at Scale & Ecosystem Entry

**Status:** Roadmapped, not approved. No FEP prompt has been issued for this generation.

## Theme

The reference model that connects workspaces within one organization (v2.0) extends past the organization's boundary. This is [FEP-001](../../FEP/FEP-001-Product-Architecture.md) Generation 3 (Scale) reaching its full stated ambition, plus the first steps into Generation 4 (Ecosystem).

## Candidate Capabilities

- **Cross-Organization Federation ("Ferret Hub").** A vendor's, partner's, or open-source dependency's workspace becomes referenceable the same way an internal shared library's is today. Matures [CAP-11 Federation](../../FEP/capabilities/FEP-002-CAP-11-Federation.md) past org-local scale. Explicitly deferred from v2.0 — see [Deferred-Scope.md](Deferred-Scope.md).
- **Extension Marketplace (first steps).** A registry of third-party source connectors and delivery integrations, beginning the Generation 4 maturity of [CAP-09 Extensibility](../../FEP/capabilities/FEP-002-CAP-09-Extensibility.md).
- **Enterprise-scale workspace registry.** Addressing the workspace-*count* scaling problem (as distinct from per-workspace size, already handled by ARCH-001) once real usage data shows it's needed.

## Why Not Earlier

Cross-organization federation raises identity, trust, and policy questions (whose access-control decisions govern a cross-org reference?) that don't exist at org-local scale. Building this before v3's governance maturity would retrofit trust onto a federation model already in production — the exact risk [FEP-001 §8](../../FEP/FEP-001-Product-Architecture.md) flags for Federation generally.

## Open Questions Feeding This Generation

Cross-organization identity/trust model and the workspace-count scaling threshold — see [FERRET-PRODUCT-ROADMAP.md §6](../FERRET-PRODUCT-ROADMAP.md#6-research-candidates).
